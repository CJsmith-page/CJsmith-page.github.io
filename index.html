About

Business Analyst with 4+ years of overall professional experience across financial operations, enterprise systems and data platforms, and process improvement.

I work closely with business, risk, and technology teams to understand business needs, clarify requirements, identify process gaps, resolve issues, and support changes from initial discussions through testing and implementation. I enjoy working through complex business problems, bringing the right stakeholders together, and helping turn requirements into solutions that work in practice.

My background in financial operations has shaped how I approach business analysis. I place strong emphasis on evidence, data accuracy, controls, and understanding the impact of changes on business processes and outcomes.

Complemented by a master's degree, PMP certification, and Lean Six Sigma training, I bring a structured approach to requirements analysis, process improvement, project delivery, and business change.
